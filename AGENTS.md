## Safety Research (Nov 2026)
- **Germany overall**: US Level 2, UK Level 1, Ireland "normal precautions", Canada Level 2
- **Terrorism**: Germany upgraded to "high threat level" July 2026 — but this is national policy, not city-specific
- **Munich**: LOW-threat for crime (US OSAC), safest major city in Germany (7 years running), Numbeo top-5 safest in Europe
- **Berlin**: AVOID — multiple protests Nov 14, 20, 25; MEDIUM threat; elevated terrorism risk
- **Nov 20 protests**: "Gen Z regelt" youth protests in 34 cities incl. Munich (Nov 20) — avoid city center that day
- **Hessen/Giessen**: AVOID — violent protests (from Nov 2025 pattern, monitor for 2026)
- **Christmas markets**: Enhanced security but safe — Munich Christkindlmarkt opens Nov 20, Nuremberg Christkindlesmarkt opens Nov 27

## Route decision
- Munich chosen over Berlin (safety), Rhine (too far from Dublin, closer to protests)
- Bavaria is safest region — Munich + Nuremberg both in Bavaria
- Nuremberg Christmas market (Nov 27) actually after trip — so plan focuses on Munich Christmas markets (open from Nov 20)
