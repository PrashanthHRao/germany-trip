Kuhfluchtwasserfälle ★ Must-See
Germany's tallest waterfall group! Three cascading tiers plunging a total of 270 meters through the Ester Mountains. The short family loop is 3.9 km (~1h15) with the lower sections on flat forest paths (stroller-friendly) and upper sections rocky (carrier needed). Free entry, open year-round. In November, expect powerful water flow from autumn rain. Source: outdoormunich.com + alltrails.com

✅️ Why Munich (not Berlin or Rhine Valley)

  • Safest city: Munich tops Germany's police safety stats for 7 consecutive years
  • LOW crime threat: US OSAC rates Munich LOW vs Berlin/Hamburg/Frankfurt MEDIUM
  • Nov 20 protest is one-day only: "Gen Z regelt" demo in Munich city center on our arrival day, afternoon/evening. Airport transfer and sights are unaffected.
  • Baby infrastructure: Wide sidewalks, smooth paving, lifts at all major stations, baby-changing at airports and train stations
  • Bavaria = safest state: Lowest crime rates in Germany overall

💡 Tips

  • Weather: November Munich is 2–8°C, likely rain. Pack waterproof layers, warm hats for baby and adults, waterproof stroller cover
  • Neuschwanstein: Book castle tour online WEEKS ahead. Arrive early. The Marienbrücke walk is steep (20 min each way) — bring baby carrier. If foggy, the castle view may be obscured.
  • Zugspitze (Top of Germany): Cable cars closed Nov 23–26 for maintenance. Partnachklamm Gorge in Garmisch is open (€10) if you want an alternative.
  • Book ahead: Neuschwanstein castle tour, GOP musical tickets, restaurants
  • Currency: Euros (€). Most places accept cards, keep €50–100 cash for markets
  • Passports: Carry them (Irish citizens need valid passport for Germany). Store baby's passport copy digitally
  • EU Health Insurance Card: Get one for baby before traveling — free at any Irish pharmacy
  • Best photo light: Dark by 16:00 in November. Do outdoor sights before 15:00, indoor after
  • Stroller storage: Trains have dedicated spaces, S-Bahn has luggage areas. Lightweight umbrella stroller recommended
