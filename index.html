⚠️ Safety Briefing

  • Munich is Germany's safest major city (top of police stats 7 years running) and rated LOW-threat by US OSAC. The right choice for a family with a baby.
  • Avoid Berlin — multiple protests on Nov 14, 20, 25 including youth "Gen Z regelt" demos with reported violence. MEDIUM-threat location.
  • Nov 20 (Thursday): "Gen Z regelt" protests in Munich city center. Day 1 is Sat Nov 22 — well clear of protest dates.
  • Germany national terror level = "high" (upgraded July 2026) — standard national posture. Enhanced security at Christmas markets with visible police. No specific threats to Munich.
  • Petty crime: Watch for pickpockets at Hauptbahnhof. Keep passports/baby documents in hotel safe.
  • Stroller-friendly: Munich has wide sidewalks, smooth paving, lifts at stations. Most attractions are accessible.

Kuhfluchtwasserfälle ★ Spectacle
Germany's tallest waterfall group! Three cascading tiers plunging a total of 270 meters through the Ester Mountains. The short family loop is 3.9 km (~1h15) with the lower sections on flat forest paths (stroller-friendly) and upper sections rocky (carrier needed). Free entry, open year-round. The forest adventure trail (Walderlebnispfad) has information stations and viewing platforms. In November, expect powerful water flow from autumn rain. Source: outdoormunich.com + alltrails.com

  • Safest city: Munich tops Germany's police safety stats for 7 consecutive years
  • LOW crime threat: US OSAC rates Munich LOW vs Berlin/Hamburg/Frankfurt MEDIUM

💡 Tips

  • Weather: November Munich is 2–8°C, likely rain. Pack waterproof layers, warm hats for baby and adults, waterproof stroller cover
  • Neuschwanstein: Book castle tour online WEEKS ahead. Arrive early. The Marienbrücke walk is steep (20 min each way) — bring baby carrier. If foggy, the castle view from the bridge may be obscured.
  • Zugspitze (Top of Germany): Germany's highest peak (2,962m) is ~1.5h by train. Cable cars closed Nov 23–26 for maintenance. Partnachklamm Gorge in Garmisch is open (€10, 8–18) if you want to swap Kuhflucht for it.
  • Book ahead: Gratitude (birthday dinner), Neuschwanstein castle tour, GOP musical tickets
  • Currency: Euros (€). Most places accept cards, but keep €50–100 cash for markets and small purchases
  • Passports: Carry them (Irish citizens need valid passport for Germany). Store baby's passport copy digitally
  • EU Health Insurance Card: Get one for the baby before traveling — free at any Irish pharmacy
  • Best photo light: Dark by 16:00 in November. Do outdoor sights before 15:00, museums/theatre after dark
  • Stroller storage: Trains have dedicated spaces, S-Bahn has luggage areas. Consider a lightweight umbrella stroller
